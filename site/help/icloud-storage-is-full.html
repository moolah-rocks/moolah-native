iCloud storage is full

A banner across the top of the window reads iCloud storage is full. Some changes can't sync until you free up space. Here's what it means and what to do.

What's happening

Your iCloud quota is full. Moolah's changes are still saved locally on this device, but they can't upload to iCloud until there's room. Sync resumes on its own once space is available — there's nothing to flip back on in Moolah.

Free up iCloud storage

On your Mac, open System Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, select your Apple Account at the top, then iCloud > Manage Account Storage. You'll see what's using your quota — usually iCloud Photos, iCloud Drive, or device backups — and you can delete what you don't need.

Upgrade your iCloud+ plan

If you'd rather keep what's there, upgrade your iCloud+ plan from the same screen.

What happens next

Once iCloud has room, Moolah's pending changes upload in the background and the banner clears. Your other devices catch up the next time they open the app.

If the banner stays

If the banner is still there a few minutes after you've freed up space, quit and reopen Moolah. If it persists, report it at github.com/ajsutton/moolah-native/issues.
