Sidebar totals

Reference for the five totals Moolah shows at the foot of the sidebar.

What each total means

All totals are shown in your profile's currency. When a rate provider can't convert a holding to the profile currency, the affected total shows as unavailable rather than reporting a partial number.

Total Definition When it shows
Current Total Sum of balances across your Bank Account, Credit Card, and Asset accounts. Always shown.
Investment Total Sum of values across your Investment, Crypto Wallet, and Exchange accounts. Shown when you have at least one investment-style account.
Earmarked Total Sum of the balances set aside in all earmarks. Shown when you have at least one earmark with a balance.
Available Funds Current Total minus Earmarked Total — the money in current accounts that isn't already set aside. Shown when Earmarked Total is positive.
Net Worth Current Total plus Investment Total. Always shown.

Currency conversion

Holdings in a currency other than your profile currency are converted using rates Moolah fetches in the background. If a rate isn't available, the total shows as unavailable. Moolah doesn't sum only the convertible portion, because that would misreport the figure.

Hidden accounts and earmarks

Hidden accounts don't contribute to any total unless you've turned on View > Show Hidden Accounts. Hidden earmarks are excluded from Earmarked Total in the same way.
