Add an investment account

Create an investment account for a brokerage, super, or retirement holding, and choose how Moolah tracks its value over time.

Before you start

Decide how you want to track value:

  • Recorded value suits accounts where you check a balance now and then — super funds, employer share plans, anything you don't trade often.
  • Calculated from trades suits accounts where you make buys, sells, and swaps you want recorded line by line. Moolah works out positions and current value from those trades at market prices.

You can switch modes later from Edit Account if you change your mind.

Steps

  1. Select + to open the New Account form. (On Mac you can also press ⌃⌘N.)
  2. Choose Investment for the account type.
  3. Enter a Name for the account.
  4. Set the account's Currency. This is the currency the value is reported in.
  5. Choose a Valuation mode: Recorded value or Calculated from trades.
  6. For Recorded value, enter an Opening Balance and Opening Date for the value you have at hand.
  7. For Calculated from trades, leave the opening balance at zero. You'll record your existing holdings as trades next.
  8. Select Create.

What's next

For Recorded value, open the account and add a value snapshot whenever you check the balance. See Record an investment value snapshot.

For Calculated from trades, record each buy, sell, or swap as a trade transaction. See Record a trade. Positions and gain/loss appear on the account view once you have at least one trade.

Result

The new account appears in the sidebar under Investments, and its value contributes to the Investment Total at the foot of the sidebar.
