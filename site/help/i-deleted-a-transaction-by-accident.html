I deleted a transaction by accident

You deleted a transaction and want it back. Here's where things stand.

There's no undo for deletions

Moolah doesn't keep a recoverable copy of a deleted transaction. Once you confirm the deletion, it's gone. The confirmation dialog says This action cannot be undone for that reason.

Re-enter it from memory

If you remember the details, add the transaction again. The date can be any past date — Moolah doesn't care that you're entering it after the fact.

  1. Select + Transaction in the transaction list toolbar (on Mac you can also press ⌘N or choose File > New Transaction).
  2. Set the date to when it actually happened.
  3. Fill in the account, type, amount, payee, and category as you remember them.

If you've forgotten the details, check the source — your bank statement, the merchant's receipt, your email — and copy the values across.

Check before you confirm

The confirmation dialog is your last chance to back out. When you see This action cannot be undone, that's the moment to stop and double-check you've selected the right row.
