A balance looks wrong

The number you're seeing doesn't match what you expect. Work through the checks below — most "wrong" balances are one of these.

Hidden accounts are excluded

Sidebar totals don't count hidden accounts. If you hid an account that still holds value, the totals it feeds will look light.

On Mac, choose View > Show Hidden Accounts (⇧⌘H). On iOS, toggle Show Hidden Accounts at the bottom of the sidebar. The hidden accounts reappear so you can see what they contain and unhide them if needed.

Spam transactions are filtered out

If you've marked tokens as spam, transactions involving those tokens are filtered out of balances and lists by default. The balance you see omits them on purpose.

On Mac, choose View > Show Spam Transactions to confirm what's being hidden. On iOS, toggle the same option at the bottom of the sidebar. If a token is hidden that shouldn't be, see Manage crypto tokens, discovered tokens, and spam.

A currency conversion couldn't run

For an account that holds more than one currency or asset, Moolah converts each position to the account's denominating currency. If a rate provider was unavailable for one of those positions, the affected total shows as unavailable rather than summed wrong.

If you see "—" or a missing total, that's the cause. Wait a moment and reload the view; the rates usually come back on their own. If a position is in a token Moolah doesn't have a price for, register the token in Settings > Crypto Tokens.

The sign is the opposite of what you'd expect

Moolah uses signs consistently across the app:

  • Expenses are negative; income is positive.
  • A refund is an expense with a positive amount — that's correct, not a typo.
  • A credit card's opening balance should be the amount you owe, entered as a negative number.

Open the transaction or the account's opening balance and check the sign. Flipping a sign by hand fixes most "off by double the amount" surprises.

A change from another device hasn't arrived

If you edited a transaction on your iPhone a moment ago, your Mac may not have caught up yet. Check the sync footer at the bottom of the sidebar for the last-synced time. If sync is paused — for example because iCloud storage is full — recent changes won't have landed.

If none of the above helped

Report it at github.com/ajsutton/moolah-native/issues with the account name, the value you see, and the value you expect.
