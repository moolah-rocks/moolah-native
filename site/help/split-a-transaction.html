Split a transaction across categories

When one transaction covers more than one category — a supermarket shop that's part groceries, part household, part pet food — split it so each part lands in the right place.

Before you start

  • You need an existing transaction to split, or a new one you're about to enter.
  • Splits stay on one account; they don't move money between accounts.

Steps

  1. Open the transaction. On a Mac, select it in the list so it appears in the inspector. On iPhone or iPad, tap it to open the detail sheet.
  2. Change Type to Custom. On a Mac you can press ⌥⌘5.
  3. Add a leg for each part of the split. Each leg has its own amount, category, and optional earmark.
  4. Repeat until you've covered the whole transaction. The form shows the running sum so you can see what's left to allocate.

Tips

  • The leg amounts should sum to the total you actually spent or received. Moolah doesn't auto-balance the legs for you.
  • You can mix earmarks across legs — for example, charge 40 of a 90 receipt against your Groceries earmark and leave the rest unassigned.
  • Signs follow the same rule as a single-leg transaction. A receipt that includes a refund alongside the purchases can have one positive leg and the rest negative.

Undo a split

To undo a split, change the type back from Custom to the single-leg type that fits (Income or Expense). Moolah keeps the first leg and drops the rest. The dropped legs are gone permanently — there is no undo. Do not switch the type until you have moved any details you want to keep (amount, category, earmark, note) onto the first leg.

Result

The transaction shows the total on one row in the list. Each split leg counts under its own category in reports and against its own earmark in the sidebar totals.
