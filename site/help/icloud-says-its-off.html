iCloud says it's off

The welcome screen shows an iCloud-off chip with a reason. Here's what each reason means and how to clear it.

iCloud is not signed in

This is the most common reason. iCloud isn't signed in at the system level on this device, so Moolah has nothing to sync to.

  1. Select Open System Settings on the welcome screen chip. (On iOS the link reads Settings.)
  2. Sign in to iCloud with your Apple ID.
  3. Return to Moolah. The chip clears and sync starts on its own.

If you have more than one device, sign in with the same Apple ID on each so your profiles travel between them.

iCloud is restricted

Something at the OS level is preventing apps from using iCloud — typically Screen Time, parental controls, or an MDM profile on a managed device.

On your Mac, check System Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy > Apps. On iOS, check Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions. Allow iCloud, then relaunch Moolah.

On a work-managed device, you may need to contact whoever administers it.

iCloud is temporarily unavailable

Apple's iCloud service is reachable but not responding right now. Nothing's wrong with your setup.

Wait a few minutes and reopen Moolah. If the chip is still there after an hour or so, restart the device.

iCloud is not available in this build

The build of Moolah you're running doesn't have iCloud entitlements. This usually means a development or internal build, not an App Store release.

Install Moolah from the App Store and open your profile there. The App Store build has the entitlements iCloud needs.

If none of these helped

Report it at github.com/ajsutton/moolah-native/issues, including the exact wording of the chip and which device you're on.
