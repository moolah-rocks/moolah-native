Add an investment account

Create an investment account for a brokerage, super, or retirement holding. Moolah calculates its positions and current value from the trades you record.

Before you start

Have the details of your existing holdings ready. After creating the account, record each buy, sell, or swap as a trade.

Steps

  1. Select + to open the New Account form. (On Mac you can also press ⌃⌘N.)
  2. Choose Investment for the account type.
  3. Enter a Name for the account.
  4. Set the account's Currency. This is the currency used to report its value.
  5. Leave the Opening Balance at zero. You'll record your existing holdings as trades next.
  6. Select Create.

Result

The new account appears in the sidebar under Investments, and its value contributes to the Investment Total at the foot of the sidebar.

Next steps

Record each buy, sell, or swap as a trade transaction. See Record a trade. Positions and gain/loss appear on the account view once you have at least one trade.
