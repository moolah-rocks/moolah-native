Record a transfer between accounts

Use a transfer when money moves between two of your own accounts — paying a credit card from a bank account, shifting savings, or moving funds between fiat and a crypto wallet.

Steps

  1. Add a new transaction (⌘N on a Mac, or + in the toolbar on iPhone or iPad).
  2. Set Type to Transfer. On a Mac you can press ⌥⌘3.
  3. Pick the From account (the source) and the To account (the destination).
  4. Enter the amount.
  5. Set the date and, if you want, a note.

Moolah records the transfer as one transaction with two legs, one on each account. It appears in both accounts' histories without double-counting.

Cross-currency transfers

When the source and destination accounts have different currencies, Moolah adds a second amount field. Enter the sent amount in the source account's currency and the received amount in the destination account's currency.

Both numbers matter. The sent amount comes out of the source account at its currency. The received amount goes into the destination account at its currency. Together they record the real-world exchange rate you got on the day — Moolah doesn't make one up.

If your bank charged a fee in either currency, fold it into the relevant side. Say you sent 1,000 in your source currency and only 935 reached the conversion: enter 935 as the sent amount. Enter the figure that actually arrived in the destination currency as the received amount. Use the note field to record any detail you want to remember.

Merge two transactions as a transfer

Sometimes you'll already have two single-account transactions that are really two halves of the same transfer — for example, you imported a withdrawal from your bank and a matching deposit into your savings. You can merge them.

  1. In the transaction list, select both rows.
  2. On a Mac, choose Transaction > Merge as Transfer. On iPhone or iPad — or on any platform — you may also see a suggestion banner when Moolah spots a likely match; select the banner to merge.

Moolah turns the two transactions into one transfer with two legs.

Undo a merge

If you merged something that shouldn't be a transfer, open the merged transaction and choose Transaction > Split Back into Separate Transactions. You're returned to the two original single-account transactions.

Result

The transfer appears in both accounts. The source account's balance drops by the sent amount; the destination account's balance rises by the received amount.
