Supported crypto chains and data providers

The chains Moolah can sync a wallet on, and the third-party services that supply on-chain data, exchange data, and prices. All of these calls go from your device directly to the provider — never via a Moolah server.

Supported chains

ChainNative assetBlock explorer
EthereumETHetherscan.io

BaseETHbasescan.org

Not supported

Moolah doesn't sync wallets on Polygon, Arbitrum, Avalanche, or Bitcoin. You can still record holdings on those chains by adding an Investment account in Recorded value mode and entering snapshots manually.
