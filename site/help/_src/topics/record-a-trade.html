Record a trade

Record a buy, sell, or swap against an investment, crypto, or exchange account. This is also how you record a stock trade.

Before you start

A trade has two main legs: what you paid and what you received. Each leg holds a currency or asset and a quantity. Optional fee legs cover brokerage and similar charges.

Moolah preserves the signs you enter. Don't flip a number to "make it negative" — enter what really moved.

Steps

  1. Select + in the transactions toolbar to open the transaction form. (On Mac you can also press ⌘N.)
  2. Set the Type to Trade. On Mac you can press ⌥⌘4.
  3. Choose the investment, crypto, or exchange Account.
  4. Set the Date and an optional Payee (such as the broker or counterparty).
  5. In the Paid leg, choose the currency or asset that left the account and enter the amount.
  6. In the Received leg, choose the currency or asset that arrived and enter the amount.
  7. If there were fees, add a Fee leg with the fee currency and amount.
  8. Add a Note if you want a reminder of what the trade was for.
  9. Select Save.

Buys, sells, swaps, and stocks

The same form covers every shape of trade:

  • Buy a stock — Paid is cash in your account's currency; Received is the stock. In the asset picker, search by ticker (such as BHP.AX or NASDAQ:AAPL).
  • Sell a stock — Paid is the stock you sold; Received is the cash you got back.
  • Swap one crypto token for another — Paid is the token you sent; Received is the token you got.
  • Buy crypto with fiat — Paid is the fiat amount; Received is the crypto.

Result

The trade appears in the account's transaction list. For Calculated-from-trades investment accounts and for crypto accounts, the positions table and the account's value update to reflect the new holding.
