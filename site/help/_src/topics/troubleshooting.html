Troubleshooting

Pick the symptom that matches what you're seeing. Each article is self-contained — there's no order to read them in.

Articles in this section

  • iCloud says it's off — the welcome screen reports iCloud as unavailable.
  • iCloud storage is full — the banner that pauses sync until you free up space.
  • Moolah won't open this profile — the Update Moolah to Continue screen.
  • My wallet or exchange account isn't syncing — Alchemy or Coinstash isn't bringing in transactions.
  • A scheduled transaction didn't post on its own — what to do when a bill comes due.
  • CSV import dropped into Needs Setup — your CSV landed in Needs Setup instead of your transaction list.
  • A balance looks wrong — the number doesn't match what you expect.
  • I deleted a transaction by accident — what to do when you delete something you wanted to keep.

If none of these fit

Open Help > Report a Bug to file an issue at github.com/moolah-rocks/moolah-native/issues. Include what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.
