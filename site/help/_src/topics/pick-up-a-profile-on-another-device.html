Pick up a profile on another device

Open a profile you created on one Apple device on another. iCloud sync does the moving for you — there's no file to copy by hand.

Before you start

  • You need both devices signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID.
  • Moolah syncs automatically once iCloud is signed in at the system level. There's nothing to enable inside the app, but it is opt-in: if iCloud is signed out, data stays on this device only.
  • You need a working internet connection on both devices for the first sync.

Steps

  1. Install Moolah from the App Store on the second device, if you haven't already.
  2. Open Moolah on the second device. The welcome screen appears.
  3. Wait while Moolah checks iCloud. You'll see Checking iCloud…, then a banner that reads We found N profiles once your profiles arrive.
  4. Select the profile you want to open. Moolah loads it and switches to it.

What to expect on the first sync

The first sync can take a moment, especially if you have many transactions. The sidebar shows a sync status footer at the bottom — it ticks through as records arrive. You can use Moolah while sync runs in the background.

After the first sync, both devices stay in step automatically. A change you make on one shows up on the other shortly after.

If profiles don't appear

If the welcome screen says iCloud is off or unavailable, check that the device is signed in to iCloud. On Mac, go to System Settings > Apple Account > iCloud. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, select your Apple Account at the top, then iCloud. See iCloud says it's off for the specific reasons and fixes.

Result

The profile is open on the second device with the same accounts, transactions, earmarks, and categories you have on the first. Changes you make on either device appear on the other.
