Edit, hide, reorder, or delete an account

Change an account's details, tuck it out of the way, move it in the sidebar, or remove it for good.

Edit an account

  1. In the sidebar, right-click the account row and select Edit Account. On iOS, swipe the row and select Edit.
  2. Change the name, currency, opening balance, opening date, or type-specific fields.
  3. Select Save.

The account type itself can't be changed after the account is created.

Hide an account

Hiding an account keeps its history but takes it out of the sidebar and the default totals. Useful for closed accounts you want to keep on the record.

  1. Open Edit Account on the account you want to hide.
  2. Turn on the Hidden toggle. The toggle is only available when the account's balance is zero.
  3. Select Save.

To see hidden accounts again, choose View > Show Hidden Accounts on Mac (⇧⌘H), or use the sidebar's hidden-accounts toggle on iOS.

Reorder accounts

On Mac:

  1. In the sidebar, drag an account row to its new position within its group.

On iOS:

  1. In the sidebar, select Edit.
  2. Drag the handle on an account row to reorder it.

You can't move an account between the Current Accounts and Investments groups — the group is fixed by the account type.

Delete an account

Deleting an account also deletes every transaction on it. There is no undo.

  1. Transfer or remove the remaining money so the account's balance is zero.
  2. Open Edit Account and turn on the Hidden toggle.
  3. Select Save.
  4. Open Edit Account on the hidden account.
  5. Select Delete Account.
  6. Confirm the warning. This action cannot be undone.

Result

Edits save straight away. A hidden account disappears from the sidebar until you toggle Show Hidden Accounts. A reordered account stays in its new spot. A deleted account is gone, along with every transaction recorded against it.
