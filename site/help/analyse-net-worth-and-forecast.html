Analyse net worth and forecast upcoming months

Use the Analysis view to see your net worth over time, break down your spending, and forecast the next few months from your scheduled transactions.

Open Analysis

  1. In the sidebar, select Analysis. On Mac, press ⌘5.

The view loads with the net worth graph, an Upcoming card, an income and expense table, an expense breakdown, and a categories-over-time chart.

Change the history window

  1. In the toolbar, open Filters.
  2. Use the History pickers to choose how far back the view looks.

The graph and tables redraw to match the new window.

Forecast upcoming months

  1. In the toolbar, open Filters.
  2. Use the Forecast picker to choose None, 1 month, 3 months, or 6 months.

Forecasts come from your scheduled transactions. If you don't have any scheduled, the forecast extends flat from today — there's nothing for Moolah to project. The more completely you've recorded upcoming income and bills as scheduled transactions, the more useful the forecast becomes. See Scheduled transactions for how to set them up.

Add a scheduled transaction from Analysis

  1. In the Analysis toolbar, select +. On Mac, press ⌘N while Analysis is focused.

Moolah creates a monthly scheduled placeholder you can edit with real values. This is a quick way to add a recurring item that you noticed missing while reading the forecast.

What the cards show

The net worth graph shows current accounts plus investment-like accounts combined into a single line. The Upcoming card lists scheduled transactions falling within the forecast window. The expense breakdown groups outgoings by category. The categories-over-time chart shows how spending in each category has moved.

Result

You see net worth across your chosen history window, with an optional forecast extending up to six months ahead based on your scheduled transactions.
