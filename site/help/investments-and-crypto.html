Investments and crypto

Moolah tracks investments, on-chain crypto wallets, and exchange accounts alongside your everyday money, with on-chain and exchange data pulled directly to your device where supported.

Three account types

Three of Moolah's account types live in this section. They all feed the Investment Total at the foot of the sidebar.

  • Investment — a brokerage or retirement account. You enter values yourself, or let Moolah work the value out from trades you record.
  • Crypto Wallet — an on-chain wallet on Ethereum, OP Mainnet, or Base. Moolah pulls transactions from Alchemy directly to your device.
  • Exchange — a Coinstash account. Moolah pulls trades from Coinstash directly to your device, using a read-only API token you create.

How an investment account tracks value

Investment accounts have two valuation modes. You choose one when you create the account, and you can change it later from Edit Account.

  • Recorded value — you note the account's value from time to time. Moolah uses your snapshots in the net worth graph.
  • Calculated from trades — you record buys, sells, and swaps as trade transactions. Moolah works out positions and the current value at market prices.

How crypto wallet sync works

A crypto wallet account holds a 0x address on one of the supported chains. Moolah queries Alchemy directly from your device for on-chain transactions, with Blockscout as a fallback. The Alchemy API key is yours — you create it on Alchemy and paste it into Moolah. Token prices come from CoinGecko (and CryptoCompare or Binance as fallbacks). Moolah doesn't sit in the middle of any of these calls.

How exchange sync works

An exchange account uses a read-only API token you create in your Coinstash account. The token lives in iCloud Keychain on your devices. Moolah queries Coinstash directly from your device for your trade history.
