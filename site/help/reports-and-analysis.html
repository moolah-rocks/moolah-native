Reports and analysis

Reports and Analysis are two separate views — one shows where your money has been, the other where it's heading.

Reports

Reports shows income and expense by category for a date range you choose. Select a row to drill into the underlying transactions.

Use Reports when you want to answer questions like "what did I spend on groceries last quarter" or "where did this year's income come from".

Analysis

Analysis shows your net worth over time, an Upcoming card for scheduled transactions, an income and expense table, an expense breakdown, and a categories-over-time chart. History pickers control how far back the view looks. Forecast pickers extend it forward by one, three, or six months.

Use Analysis when you want to see trends, plan the next few months, or check the shape of your net worth.

How the two views relate

Reports is a look-back at categorised spending. Analysis combines history with forecasting. Forecasts in Analysis come from your scheduled transactions, so the value of the forecast depends on how completely you've recorded what's coming up.

Both views respect filters where they apply, so account, earmark, and category choices in your sidebar carry through.
