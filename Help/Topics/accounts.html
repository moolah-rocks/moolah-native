Accounts

An account in Moolah represents a real-world place you keep money or assets, such as a bank account, credit card, or crypto wallet.

What an account is

Every transaction you record belongs to an account. The sidebar lists your accounts, grouped by whether they hold spendable money or longer-term investments. Each account has a name, a denominating currency, and an opening balance.

The six account types

Moolah supports six account types. You pick the type when you create the account, and you can't change it later.

  • Bank Account — a chequing, savings, or transaction account.
  • Credit Card — a revolving credit account. The balance is what you owe, so it's recorded as a negative number.
  • Asset — anything else of value you want to track, like a car or a property.
  • Investment — a brokerage or retirement account. You choose whether to record values manually or let Moolah calculate them from trades.
  • Crypto Wallet — an on-chain wallet on Ethereum, OP Mainnet, or Base. Transactions auto-import once you've added an Alchemy API key.
  • Exchange — a Coinstash exchange account. Trades auto-import using a read-only API token you create on Coinstash.

Current accounts and investments

The sidebar splits accounts into two groups. Bank, credit card, and asset accounts are your current accounts: spendable money and day-to-day assets. Investment, crypto wallet, and exchange accounts are grouped as investments: holdings whose value tends to move with the market.

This grouping drives the totals at the foot of the sidebar. Current accounts feed the Current Total; investment-style accounts feed the Investment Total.

Currency

Each account has its own currency. For a crypto wallet, this is the wallet's native currency (ETH on the chains Moolah supports). You can hold accounts in different currencies inside one profile, and Moolah converts to your profile currency for the sidebar totals.
