Pay a scheduled transaction

Mark a scheduled transaction as done so it posts to your account — and, if it's recurring, advances to the next occurrence.

Before you start

  • Moolah doesn't post scheduled transactions automatically — you confirm each one yourself.
  • You can pay it on its scheduled date, earlier, or later.
  • You can adjust the date or amount before paying if reality didn't match the schedule.

Steps

You can pay a scheduled transaction from three places. Use whichever fits what you're doing.

From transaction detail

  1. Open the scheduled transaction from the Scheduled sidebar item or from Upcoming.
  2. If the date or amount differs from what actually happened, edit them first.
  3. Select Pay Now.

From the list

  1. In the Scheduled or Upcoming list, find the row.
  2. Use the row's pay action — swipe on iOS, or use the context menu on Mac.

From the menu bar (Mac)

  1. Select the scheduled transaction in the list.
  2. Choose Transaction > Pay Scheduled Transaction.

Adjusting before you pay

Real bills rarely arrive for the exact amount on the exact day. Before you select Pay Now, change the Date to when the money actually moved, and change the Amount to what was actually charged. Pay Now then records the real values, not the scheduled ones.

What happens next

If the transaction recurs, Moolah advances it to its next scheduled date based on its recurrence. "Every month" rolls it forward by a month. "Every 2 weeks" rolls it forward by a fortnight. The original transaction is now posted, and a new scheduled occurrence takes its place.

If the transaction is one-off (Recurrence set to Once), it leaves the scheduled list entirely.

Result

The transaction posts to its account and shows up in the main transaction list with the date and amount you confirmed.
