Welcome to Moolah

Moolah is personal finance for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. You enter what you spend and earn; Moolah keeps track. Set it up. Then go live your life.

What Moolah is

Moolah is a native app for tracking your money by hand. You record transactions, group them into categories, and set aside funds in earmarks. Reports and charts then show you where your money goes.

There's no sign-up and no Moolah account. Your data lives on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, it moves between your devices through your own iCloud — no Moolah servers in the middle. See Sync and privacy for the full picture.

Where to start

If you've installed Moolah recently, head to Getting started. It walks you from the welcome screen to a usable profile in a few minutes. From there, Accounts covers adding the places you keep your money.
