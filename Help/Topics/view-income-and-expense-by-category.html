View income and expense by category

Use the Reports view to see where money came in and where it went across a date range, grouped by category.

Open Reports

  1. In the sidebar, select Reports. On Mac, press ⌘4.

The view opens with a date range and a table of income and expense totals by category.

Choose a date range

  1. In the toolbar, select the Date Range picker.
  2. Choose a preset such as This Financial Year, Last month, Last 12 months, Year to date, or one of the other presets.
  3. To set your own window, choose Custom. The From and To date pickers appear; set both.

The table updates to show totals for the new range.

Read the table

Income and expense are shown separately in the same screen. Each row is a category, with a total for the range. Parent categories roll up their children, so a parent row reflects spending across all its sub-categories.

Drill into a category

  1. Select a row in the report.

Moolah opens the transaction list filtered to that category and date range. From there, refine further with search or the filter sheet.

Result

You see income and expense by category for the range you picked, and you can step into any category to see the transactions behind the total.
