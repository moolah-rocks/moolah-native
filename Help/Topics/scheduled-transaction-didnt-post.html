A scheduled transaction didn't post on its own

You set up a scheduled transaction. The date rolled around. Your balance didn't change. Here's why.

Moolah doesn't post them for you

Scheduled transactions don't post on their own. When one comes due, you select Pay Now to record it. That's not a bug — it's how Moolah is designed to work.

Real bills rarely match a schedule exactly. The rent went up, the direct debit ran a day late, the streaming service charged a different amount. Pay Now lets you adjust the date and amount to what actually happened, then post the real values.

Pay it now

  1. Open Scheduled or Upcoming in the sidebar.
  2. Select the transaction.
  3. If the date or amount differs from reality, edit them first.
  4. Select Pay Now.

For a recurring transaction, paying it advances the schedule to the next occurrence. For a one-off, paying it removes it from the scheduled list.

If you'd like a reminder

The Upcoming view (⌘2 on Mac) groups what's due soon, so you can see at a glance which scheduled transactions need attention.
